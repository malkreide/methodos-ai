# Start, Stop, Continue Retrospective

Three prompts that push a team from "how did that feel" to "what will we do
differently", in under an hour.

- **Start** — behaviours we should adopt
- **Stop** — behaviours costing us more than they return
- **Continue** — what is working and must survive the next change

## When to use

- End of a sprint, project phase, or quarter
- After a team composition or process change, to check what settled
- As a recurring low-ceremony habit between deeper retrospectives

## When *not* to use

- For a specific failure with a specific cause — use [Five Whys](Five_Whys.md)
- Immediately after a painful incident, while the discussion is still hot
- When the last round's Start items were never done; fix that first, or the
  format teaches the team that retrospectives are theatre

## Facilitation outline (60 min)

1. (5 min) Restate the period under review and the agreed ground rules.
2. (10 min) Silent writing, one item per note. Silence first is what keeps the
   quiet half of the team in the conversation.
3. (20 min) Post and cluster per column. Author reads their own note.
4. (15 min) Dot-vote. Take the top two or three only.
5. (10 min) Each selected item gets a named owner and a date. No owner, no item.

## Common pitfalls

- Skipping the Continue column, so working practices get optimized away
- Producing twelve action items, none of which happen
- Assigning items to "the team" rather than a person
- Running the identical format for a year until contributions go flat
