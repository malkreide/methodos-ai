# Sailboat Retrospective

One picture instead of three lists.

| Element | Means |
|---|---|
| 🏝️ Island | the goal we are sailing toward |
| 💨 Wind | what is pushing us forward |
| ⚓ Anchors | what is slowing us down |
| 🪨 Rocks | risks ahead, not yet hit |

The rocks are what list-based formats usually miss: they look backward, this
one also looks forward.

## When to use

- The team has gone quiet in list-format retrospectives
- Goal, momentum, drag and upcoming risk belong in one conversation
- Visual thinkers who contribute little in verbal rounds

## When *not* to use

- Debriefing one specific event — use [After Action Review](After_Action_Review.md)
- Audio-only sessions without a shared drawing surface
- When the team likes its current format and it still produces actions

## Facilitation outline (60 min)

1. (5 min) Draw the boat. Write the island first — the goal frames everything else.
2. (10 min) Silent writing across all four areas at once.
3. (20 min) Post and cluster. Author reads their own note.
4. (10 min) Dot-vote anchors and rocks together, not separately.
5. (15 min) Top two items get an owner and a date. Rocks get an early-warning signal.

## Common pitfalls

- Enjoying the drawing and skipping step 5
- Leaving the island blank, which makes wind and anchors unmeasurable
- Treating rocks as decoration rather than as risks with owners
- Reusing the metaphor so often it becomes the same list format with pictures
